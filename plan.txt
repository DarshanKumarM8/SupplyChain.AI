### The Stampede Index ($S_t \in [0, 100]$)

Computed identically on both market baseline and STAMPEDE dashboards using a continuous 30-second rolling window across all $N$ agents:


$$S_t = 100 \times \left[ w_1 \left( \frac{1 + \rho_{\text{Spearman}}(\mathbf{O}_t)}{2} \right) + w_2 \cdot \tilde{v}_{\text{deplete}}(t) + w_3 \cdot \tilde{\sigma}_{\text{rate}}(t) \right]$$

* **$\rho_{\text{Spearman}}(\mathbf{O}_t)$:** Mean pairwise Spearman rank correlation of order vectors $\mathbf{O}_t$ across firms, measuring directional herd alignment.
* **$\tilde{v}_{\text{deplete}}(t)$:** Normalized capacity depletion velocity of shared bottleneck nodes:

$$\tilde{v}_{\text{deplete}}(t) = \min\left(1, \; \frac{1}{K} \sum_{k=1}^K \frac{-\Delta C_k(t) / \Delta t}{C_k^{\max}}\right)$$


* **$\tilde{\sigma}_{\text{rate}}(t)$:** Min-max normalized realized volatility of spot lane freight rates across primary logistics corridors over the rolling window.
* **Weights:** Set statically to $w_1 = 0.45$, $w_2 = 0.35$, $w_3 = 0.20$.

### Market Price Impact & Information Leakage (Telegraph Physics)

When firms book backup capacity $Q_{\text{booked}}$ against available lane depth $D_{\text{available}}$, market spot prices move via a non-linear Kyle/Grossman-Stiglitz impact function:


$$P_{\text{lane}}(t) = P_0 \cdot \left( 1 + \lambda \cdot \left( \frac{Q_{\text{booked}}(t)}{D_{\text{available}}(t)} \right)^\gamma \right)$$

* **Naive Booking (Telegraph):** Directly commits large spot orders, blowing out $\frac{Q}{D}$, causing a price surge ($\ge 34\%$) and signaling the shock to competitor risk bots.


* **Option Execution (STAMPEDE):** Exercises pre-negotiated bilateral capacity call options held in escrow, keeping $Q_{\text{spot}} \approx 0$ and spot price delta flat ($\le 4\%$).

### Buffer-Diversity Score & Phased Release (Anti-Synchronization)

To prevent the "synchronized late-mover stampede" caused by homogeneous inventory buffers:


$$D_{\text{buffer}}(i) = 1 - \frac{\mathbf{b}_i \cdot \bar{\mathbf{b}}_{-i}}{\Vert{}\mathbf{b}_i\Vert{}_2 \Vert{}\bar{\mathbf{b}}_{-i}\Vert{}_2}$$

* **$\mathbf{b}_i$:** The SKU-level inventory buffer vector of firm $i$.
* **$\bar{\mathbf{b}}_{-i}$:** Average buffer holding vector of competitor peer cohort.
* **Phased Release Schedule:** Buffers are released in staggered tranches over weeks $W_1, W_3, W_5$ based on the firm's $D_{\text{buffer}}$, preventing identical exhaustion horizons.

### Rot-Aware Routing & Replacement Carbon Objective

Perishable cargo routing optimizes across the $\alpha$-quantile of kinetic decay (Value-at-Risk) and downstream Scope 3 batch-replacement emissions:


$$\min_{\text{route } r} \; \left[ \text{Cost}_{\text{freight}}(r) + \text{VaR}_\alpha(\text{Decay}_r) + \mathbb{E}\left[ \text{CO2e}_{\text{transit}}(r) + \mathbb{I}(\text{Spoil}_r) \cdot \text{CO2e}_{\text{re-produce}} \right] \right]$$

* **Kinetic Decay:** Governed by Arrhenius temperature integration along the lane telemetry profile.


* **$\mathbb{I}(\text{Spoil}_r) \cdot \text{CO2e}_{\text{re-produce}}$:** Penalizes high-variance routes where spoilage triggers an entire re-manufacture and re-shipment cycle.
